# Fact-Checking Guide

## Verifying Public Statements

When fact-checking a statement attributed to a public figure, follow these steps:

1. **Source Verification**
   - Find the original source of the quote
   - Check the full context of the statement
   - Verify the date and location of the statement
   - Cross-reference with official records or transcripts

2. **Media Coverage Analysis**
   - Check reputable news sources
   - Look for video or audio recordings
   - Compare different media outlets' reporting
   - Note any corrections or retractions

3. **Historical Context**
   - Research the background of the topic
   - Understand the timeline of events
   - Check for previous related statements
   - Consider the broader context

4. **Expert Consultation**
   - Identify subject matter experts
   - Review academic or professional analysis
   - Check fact-checking organizations
   - Consider multiple expert perspectives

## Best Practices

1. **Documentation**
   - Keep detailed records of sources
   - Save screenshots or archives
   - Note the verification process
   - Document contradictions found

2. **Bias Awareness**
   - Check multiple perspectives
   - Be aware of your own biases
   - Consider political or social context
   - Look for agenda-driven reporting

3. **Common Pitfalls**
   - Misattributed quotes
   - Out-of-context statements
   - Edited or manipulated content
   - Satire mistaken for fact

4. **Reporting Findings**
   - Present evidence clearly
   - Acknowledge uncertainties
   - Provide source citations
   - Update if new information emerges
